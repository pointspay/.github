# Pointspay 

Pointspay is an innovative marketing and payment technology, 
available to leading online shops worldwide. 
With Pointspay, members can spend and collect points or miles from their favorite loyalty programs. 
You can take advantage of incredible offers from participating online shops and loyalty programs, specially curated for Pointspay users.
